# Product Image Visual Mapping Notes

## Evidence and scope

These notes are based on the owner-supplied product photographs. They identify only what is visually safe to state for navigation and accessibility. They do not establish price, material, purity, gemstone, certification, size, stock, product name, or sales status. Every published item remains **unpriced** and requires business review before a final product identity is public.

## Batch 01 observations

The first batch contains small gold-tone jewellery close-ups on pale fabric, with several blue- and red-fabric variants. Most early images are too tightly framed to assign a reliable retail category; they should use neutral labels such as **“Jewellery detail — product record in preparation”** until the owner confirms the item identity. The batch also includes a visually clear fine chain/bracelet-style image, an adjustable bracelet-style image, and a long necklace-style image. These may be assigned temporary visual categories of **bracelets** or **necklaces** only for browsing, with final taxonomy requiring review.

> Publishing standard: Alt text must state only visible form, color tone, and setting. It must not call an item gold, diamond, pearl, certified, handmade, or a particular product type unless a confirmed record supports that description.
