Refund & Returns

Not the right piece? We make it right — simply message us on WhatsApp within 15 days of delivery.

Home›Refund & Returns
15
Day Refund Promise
If you're not satisfied, notify us within 15 days of delivery — no questions, no forms, no runaround.
⚡ Our Commitment

We are a small, family-run business. Every order is handled personally by our family team. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase — and if you're not, we want to make it right as quickly and simply as possible.

There are no complicated forms, no return labels to print, and no lengthy processing delays. Just WhatsApp us.

Who Can Request a Refund?

  • You received a different product than what you ordered
  • The product arrived damaged or defective
  • The product does not match the description on our website
  • You changed your mind (within 15 days of delivery — see conditions below)

Important for change-of-mind returns: The product must be unused, in its original condition, and returned within 15 days of delivery. Courier charges and COD fees are non-refundable for change-of-mind requests — only the product price is refunded.

The Refund Process

Simple, WhatsApp-based — no online portal, no forms to fill in. We keep it human.

  • Step 1: Message us on WhatsApp

    Send a message to +880 1740-501062 with your order details (or the product name/page link) and a brief description of the issue. If it's a damaged product, please share a clear photo.

  • Step 2: We confirm your request

    We'll acknowledge your message, review your case, and confirm whether a refund or exchange is applicable. Our team responds quickly — usually within the same working day.

  • Step 3: Return the product via Pathao Courier

    If your request is approved, we provide a Pathao courier booking to return the product to us. The return courier charge is paid by the customer (unless the issue is our fault — e.g., wrong/damaged product). We coordinate the booking via WhatsApp.

  • Step 4: Refund issued

    Once we receive and inspect the returned product, we process your refund. Refunds are issued via bKash or Nagad to the same number you used to place the order. Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days of receiving the returned item.

What Is Not Eligible for Refund?

  • Products returned after 15 days from the delivery date
  • Used, altered, or damaged by the customer (not due to shipping)
  • Products returned without prior WhatsApp notification
  • Items purchased during a sale or promotion (unless defective)
  • Courier charges and COD fees (non-refundable under any circumstance)

Refund Timeline

  • 15-day window: Notify us within 15 days of delivery
  • Return window: Return the product within 5 working days of approval
  • Processing: 3–5 working days after we receive the returned item
  • Total maximum time: Approximately 20–25 working days from initial notification to refund in your bKash/Nagad

Exchange Option

Instead of a refund, you can request an exchange for a different product of the same or higher value (paying the difference). Exchanges are subject to availability and are handled the same way — just start with a WhatsApp message.

eMarket247 vs. Pathao Courier Responsibilities

eMarket247 is responsible for the quality of the product and accurate description. Pathao Courier is responsible for the safe transit of your parcel. If your parcel is lost or severely damaged during shipping, we will work with Pathao to resolve the issue — but the final liability for transit lies with Pathao as the logistics provider.

We recommend inspecting your parcel upon delivery before paying the courier. If the parcel appears visibly damaged or tampered with, refuse it and notify us immediately via WhatsApp.

Message us on WhatsApp — we'll guide you through the process personally.
