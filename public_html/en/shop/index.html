Buying Answers

How to Choose Jewellery Online

How do I choose jewellery online?

Start with the type of jewellery you want, then consider the occasion, outfit, style and the available product details. Checking the product information before ordering can help you choose with greater confidence.

What types of jewellery can I find at eMarket247?

eMarket247 offers jewellery across categories such as earrings, bangles, bracelets, necklaces, pendants, rings, jewellery sets and gift jewellery, subject to the products currently available in the collection.

What should I check before buying jewellery online?

Check the product name, jewellery type, materials or finish where provided, size or dimension information where available, the price, the care information on the product page, and our ordering and return policies before purchasing. Returns are covered by our 15-day refund promise.
