Delivery & Shipping

Fast, trackable doorstep delivery across Bangladesh via Pathao Courier — reliable logistics backed by real-time tracking and WhatsApp support.

Home›Delivery
⚡ Quick Answer

We ship to all 64 districts in Bangladesh via Pathao Courier. Standard delivery takes 24–72 hours depending on location. Your order is trackable from the moment it leaves our hands.

Courier charges are charged separately at delivery — they are not included in the product price. This keeps our listed prices transparent and honest.

How It Works

  • Order via WhatsApp — Send us the product name or page link. We'll confirm price, availability, and courier charge.
  • Order confirmed — We process and hand your parcel to Pathao Courier, typically within 1 working day.
  • Parcel dispatched — You receive a Pathao tracking number via WhatsApp SMS.
  • Delivered to doorstep — Pathao delivers within 24–72 hours depending on your district. You pay the courier charge + any COD fee upon receipt.

Delivery Charges

Pathao charges are weight-based and route-based. As a general guide:

Route Type Delivery Time Estimated Charge
Inside Dhaka (Same-city) 24 hours BDT 60 – 90
Outside Dhaka (Inter-city) 48 – 72 hours BDT 110 – 180
Remote / Hard-to-reach areas Up to 5 days BDT 180 – 250

Exact courier charge is confirmed before dispatch. Charges are non-negotiable per Pathao's rate card.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

We accept Cash on Delivery. COD fees apply:

  • COD fee: 0.5% – 1% of the product order value (charged by Pathao, not eMarket247)
  • Example: On a BDT 1,500 order, COD fee = approximately BDT 7.50 – 15
  • COD fee is added to the courier charge and collected upon delivery
  • You can also pay by bKash or Nagad to the courier rider upon delivery to avoid the COD fee

Tracking Your Order

Once your parcel is dispatched, we share the Pathao tracking number via WhatsApp. You can track your delivery in real time using:

  • The Pathao app (download from Google Play or App Store)
  • The Pathao website: pathao.com
  • By replying to our WhatsApp message — we track it for you if needed

Pathao also sends SMS updates to your mobile number at each status change (picked up, in transit, out for delivery, delivered).

Delivery to Remote Areas

Pathao covers all 64 districts of Bangladesh. For remote thanas or areas outside their standard delivery network, Pathao may:

  • Arrange delivery via a partner courier with a longer timeframe
  • Connect you with a nearby pickup point
  • Contact you directly to arrange the best option

We always confirm delivery feasibility with you before processing remote-area orders.

Who Ships the Order?

Orders are packed and dispatched by eMarket247 from our location in Kalukhetra, Yakubpur, Thakurgaon. We hand the parcel to Pathao's collection team. From that point, Pathao's delivery network takes over.

We are a small, family-run business. We do not use multiple warehouses — every order is handled personally by our family team before it goes to the courier.

Have a question about delivery to your area?

Message us on WhatsApp before ordering — we confirm delivery availability and exact charges for your exact location.
