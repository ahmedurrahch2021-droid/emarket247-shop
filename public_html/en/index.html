Care

Will the colour last?

Gold-tone jewellery is a base metal finished with gold-coloured plating. The colour lasts longest when you keep pieces dry and away from perfume, and take them off before the shower, wudu and monsoon humidity. Our care guide explains what we have actually seen happen to pieces like ours — and the four rules that make them last.

Read the care guide →

Solid gold vs gold-tone

What you are buying, in plain words.

Solid gold is 22K or 21K gold all the way through, sold by weight and priced by the day's rate. Every piece at eMarket247 is gold-tone — a base metal finished with gold-coloured plating.

That difference is why our pieces cost far less, and why they need more care. We never describe gold-tone pieces as real gold, and we state the material honestly on every product page.

FAQ

Five honest answers.

Is this real gold?

No. Every piece is gold-tone (imitation) jewellery — a base metal with gold-coloured plating, not solid gold. We say so plainly on every product page.

Will the colour fade?

Plating wears over time with friction, moisture, perfume and sweat. Kept dry and stored sealed, our gold-tone pieces hold their colour far longer. Our care guide explains what we have observed.

How do I know the price?

We publish a clear price range for each category. Message us on WhatsApp with the pieces you like and we confirm the exact price and availability before you pay.

Do you deliver to my district?

Yes — we deliver across Bangladesh. Delivery charges and timeframes depend on your area, so ask us for your district before you order.

Can I return it?

Yes. We offer a 15-day return window. Message us on WhatsApp to start a return and we will explain what qualifies and how the refund works.
