THE RADICAL TRUST THESIS (প্রকৃত সত্য ও আস্থার দর্শন)

In the Bangladeshi jewellery market, trust is in deep deficit. Customers are accustomed to exaggerated promises ('আজীবন গ্যারান্টি', 'হুবহু সোনা', fake discount timers) followed by parcel disappointment. Every online customer carries unspoken anxieties, silent social pressures, and past heartbreak.

eMarket 247's strategic moat is Radical Transparency: We do not compete on artificial cheapness. When we charge a premium, we openly explain the extra craftsmanship, 3-layer micro-gold plating, nickel-free skin safety, and multi-stage manual quality checks. We speak the exact inner dialogue of the customer in natural, heartfelt Bengali—transforming City Gold from a 'compromised substitute' into a proud, intelligent, and dignified lifestyle choice.

6.2 Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD Graph)
Deploy complete JSON-LD schema across the entire site ecosystem:
1. FAQPage Schema: Mandated on Homepage, Category pages, and PDPs. Directly populates SERP accordion dropdowns.
2. Product & Offer Schema: Includes priceCurrency: 'BDT', availability: 'InStock', and verified customer reviews.
3. BreadcrumbList Schema: Ensures clean breadcrumbs in search snippets (হোম › শপ › আংটি › পণ্য).
4. LocalBusiness Schema: Establishes brand entity authority with contact numbers, physical location, and founder details.
6.3 Pre-rendering for Crawler & AI Visibility (P0 Technical)
Currently, `shop.html` and `product.html` render empty containers populated via JavaScript (`পণ্য লোড হচ্ছে…`). Search bots and AI web scrapers bypass client-side JavaScript. Implement Pre-rendering or Static Site Generation (SSG) so that raw HTML responses contain full product titles, Bengali copy, specifications, and care instructions.
