Jewellery Care Standard

Preserving the finish and luster of your jewellery

  • Avoid Moisture & Chemicals: Keep away from perfumes, hairsprays, lotions, and harsh household chemicals.
  • Separate Storage: Store in a soft fabric pouch or individual compartment to prevent surface scratching.
  • Post-Wear Cleaning: Gently wipe down with a dry, soft lint-free cloth after wear to remove surface oils.
  • Remove Before Water Activity: Remove prior to showering, swimming, or vigorous physical exercise.
